## Editorial decision

Preserve the connection between repeatable content, lead handling, and decisions based on records. Lead with marketing infrastructure for established service businesses. Position AI as assistance inside an owned, documented workflow. The new carousel is educational prospect content, with a practical diagnostic and one services CTA.

## What carries over from the code

The original single-document format, ten-slide narrative, `.preview-row`, `.slide-wrapper`, `.slide`, `.headline`, brand label, `.step-counter`, `.step-pip`, and swipe-hint components remain the foundation. The fixed-size slide shell and scaled screen preview remain. Styling and copy required revision for the new aspect ratio and positioning. The new source adds embedded fonts, format switching, guides, copy editing, saved HTML, deterministic exports, and exact layer data. No framework, site deployment, or unrelated application was added.

## Evidence boundaries

The inquiry card and quote fields are labeled examples. The stage sequence illustrates a service sales workflow. It contains no fabricated client data, benchmarks, results, testimonials, forecasts, or conversion promises. The five-record diagnostic is a small operational spot check, not a statistically representative audit. Human review remains responsible for factual service details, scope, price, and promises.

Use a consistent inquiry cohort when calculating conversion. Define a consult booking once. Keep open inquiries visible so immature cohorts are not mistaken for lost sales. Record response times in one timezone and decide whether the business reports elapsed or staffed hours. The slide teaches what to measure, without presenting invented performance.

## Strategic recommendation

Use this as a focused educational carousel within Edge21's content system. The strongest audience filter is the service-business inquiry problem, rather than AI expertise or tool enthusiasm. The next related content should show a real, consented workflow improvement with baseline, date range, and outcome. Publication timing and approval status remain separate decisions.
